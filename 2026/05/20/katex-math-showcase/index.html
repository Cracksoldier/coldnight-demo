Avoiding false positives

The $...$ delimiter matches any two dollar signs on the same line, so prose containing currency can trigger the renderer unintentionally. For example, writing “costs 50andsaves50 and saves50andsaves30” would render “50 and saves” as a math expression.

To avoid this, either escape the dollar sign with a backslash (\$50) or rephrase to keep dollar signs on separate lines. Display blocks ($$...$$) are less prone to false positives since they require a blank line around them in most contexts.
